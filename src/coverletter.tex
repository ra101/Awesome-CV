\lettersection{About Me}
My name is Parth Agarwal, and I am writing to express my interest in joining your team. I am confident that I would be a strong fit for this role because of the range of projects I have been part of and my eagerness to help take your organization to the next level.

\lettersection{Relevant Experience}
At Innovaccer, I had the opportunity to work extensively with the \textbf{Django} framework and the Python ecosystem to build maintainable, readable systems. I have worked with PostgreSQL extensions such as Timescale and PostGIS, and I was often the go-to person for GitLab pipelines across our products. I also believe in building frameworks rather than writing standalone scripts, a mindset that continues to shape how I approach engineering work.

\lettersection{Why Me?}
Beyond coding, I have been recognized for my mentorship and ownership. After demonstrating initiative, I was assigned to lead the development effort for our products within our client's ecosystem. While my core strengths are in Python, backend engineering, and platform work, I am also comfortable across languages, design patterns, and product contexts, and I am excited by the opportunity to contribute at scale.
